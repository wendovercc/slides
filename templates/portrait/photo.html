{#- portrait/photo.html — the phone rendering of templates/slides/photo.html. docs/portrait-decks.md, phase 1, the half that was still open: "until it lands the pavilion's photographs are eight letterboxed steps". A FRAGMENT, not a document — no doctype, no , no styles of its own; everything it needs is in assets/css/portrait.css under `.pfrag-photo`. Same `slide` dict as the wall template. What differs is the whole reason this file exists: - THE PHOTOGRAPH STAYS WIDE. The doc's cropping rule — crop-to-fill works for people and action, wide interiors stay wide — and the pavilion is squarely the second: cropping a room shot to a phone's 9:19.5 throws away the room. So the shot keeps its native 16:9 and the step spends its remaining height on the words instead of on the picture. - AN AMBIENT GROUND. A 16:9 shot at full phone width is about a quarter of the screen, and the wall's answer to the rest — black — is what the letterbox band already gave. Here the same photograph, blurred and veiled, is the ground it sits on, so the step is lit by its own picture rather than being a band floating in a void. - THE CAPTION IS TYPE, NOT AN OVERLAY. On the wall it is a scrim over the photograph's bottom third, because a slide has one screen. A column has room below the print, where a caption can be read at phone type sizes instead of at whatever `--pfit` scaled the band's `vw` down to. `data-atom` is the structural requirement: an atom is an addressable position on the portrait surface exactly as it is a beat on the wall, and play mode (phase 5) traverses these. A photograph is one atom. -#}
