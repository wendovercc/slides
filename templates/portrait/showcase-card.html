{#- portrait/showcase-card.html — the phone rendering of templates/slides/showcase-card.html. docs/portrait-decks.md, phase 1. A FRAGMENT, not a document: no doctype, no , no styles of its own. It is fetched by portrait.js and inserted into the player document, so everything it needs is in assets/css/portrait.css under `.pfrag-showcase` — see the isolation note at the top of that file. Same `slide` dict as the wall template, and deliberately so: the words are the slide's, the layout is the surface's. Two things differ, both decided in the doc: - NO QR CODE. A QR is how you pass a URL to someone standing in front of a television; this reader is holding the phone it exists to reach. So the links are unconditional here, where the wall template hides them behind `body.interactive` whenever a code covers the wall. - The brand lockup is centred at the top rather than pinned to the top-left corner, which is where portrait.js floats the share button. `data-atom` is the structural requirement, not decoration: an atom is an addressable position on the portrait surface exactly as it is a beat on the wall, and play mode (phase 5) traverses these. A showcase card is one atom, so the fragment carries one anchor at its root. -#}
